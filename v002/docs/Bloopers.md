# Bloopers — v002

A reference repo shows you the polished result. This page shows you the bloopers reel —
the bugs we shipped to ourselves, the hours we donated to the void, and the small mercies
of catching them before anyone else did. We're laughing *with* us. (We're the only ones
here.)

The format, when entries land: a numbered act with a title, **what happened**, **the
cause**, **the fix**, and a one-line **moral**. Told honestly, with humor, the way the
[jac313 bloopers reel](https://github.com/) tells them. v002 is the **C++26** world, so
expect a few entries to be contracts-shim and bleeding-edge-toolchain flavored.

## The silver lining

Every entry here paid for a guardrail. When a blooper lands, this section records the
check, counter, or assertion it bought — so the same mistake gets caught in the first ten
seconds next time, not at hour two.
